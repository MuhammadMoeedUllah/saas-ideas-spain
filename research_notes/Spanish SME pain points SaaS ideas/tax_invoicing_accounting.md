# Tax, invoicing and accounting compliance burden on Spanish SMEs, autónomos and startups (2024–2026)

Research date: 23 October 2026. Method note for the report writer:
- The shared web-search budget ran out after six searches. The rest of the evidence comes from direct fetches of official texts (BOE, AEAT, Ministerio de Hacienda, Diputaciones Forales of Bizkaia, Gipuzkoa and Álava, Agencia Tributaria Canaria), vendor price pages, Trustpilot, and press articles found in search results or by browsing outlet section pages. Some areas are thinner as a result: Navarra, Canarias IGIC, traditional gestoría prices, Sage/a3/Zoho pricing, and Basque press coverage of shop and bar complaints. These are listed under Gaps.
- Many press facts come from one outlet, Diario Autónomos y Emprendedor (autonomosyemprendedor.es, "AyE"). It runs an "Info ATA" section carrying content from ATA, the self-employed federation, so ATA figures (hours, € costs, survey shares) should be read as advocacy data with little disclosed method.
- Several surveys are vendor-run: TeamSystem/Ipsos, Quipu (Quipu is now TeamSystem-branded), Cegid, Xolo, and Infoautónomos ENA. TeamSystem and Quipu are therefore not independent of each other.
- Every regulatory status below was checked against BOE/AEAT/Hacienda pages as of mid-to-late October 2026 unless marked otherwise.

## 1. VeriFactu (RD 1007/2023, Reglamento de requisitos de los sistemas informáticos de facturación): scope, dates, certification, costs, readiness, Excel/Word/paper

### Takeaway
The dates in force are still **1 January 2027** for Impuesto sobre Sociedades (IS) taxpayers and **1 July 2027** for everyone else (RDL 15/2025). On 5 October 2026 Hacienda announced it will move all pending obligations to **October 2028**, to coincide with B2B e-invoicing for firms with turnover up to €8M. As of 21 October 2026 that change is not in the BOE, and the AEAT FAQ still shows the 2027 dates. Software producers have had to sell compliant products since 29 July 2025. Readiness is low: depending on the survey, only 7–35% of autónomos are ready and 41% of firms confuse VeriFactu with e-invoicing. Compliant invoicing software is cheap or free (€0–€30/month). The real cost falls on retail and hospitality, which have to adapt or replace TPVs, cash registers and scales, and on firms that already paid to adapt ahead of a deadline that keeps moving.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal text in force.** The consolidated RD 1007/2023 was last updated on 03/12/2025. It has been modified by **RD 254/2025 of 1 April** (BOE-A-2025-6600) and **RDL 15/2025 of 2 December** (BOE-A-2025-24446). The current disposición final cuarta says IS taxpayers "deberán tener adaptados los sistemas informáticos… antes del 1 de enero de 2027" and the rest of the art. 3.1 obligados "antes del 1 de julio de 2027". Producers and resellers (art. 3.2) must offer adapted products within nine months of the technical ministerial order taking effect. The text contains no reference to October 2028. — [BOE, consolidated RD 1007/2023, last update 03/12/2025](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-24840)

Entities fully exempt from IS are also excluded. — [BOE consolidated RD 1007/2023](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-24840). Two secondary sources confirm the SII exclusion and also exclude Basque foral taxpayers, who use TicketBAI instead. — [VeriBai, 5 Oct 2026](https://veribai.com/calendario-verifactu) (vendor); [Webpsilon, rev. 17 Oct 2026](https://www.webpsilon.com/blog/verifactu-que-es-cuando-entra-en-vigor-y-a-quien-afecta/)

### Inferences
- Two legal states now coexist: the 2027 dates in the BOE and the October 2028 date in an official note. Until an RDL or RD is published, prudent businesses and vendors have to plan for 2027. That creates demand for "regulatory status" monitoring and plain-language guidance, which asesorías currently supply by email.
- Pure compliant invoicing is commoditised. It is free from the AEAT app, FacturaScripts and the Contasimple free tier, and costs €5–€30/month from SaaS vendors (Section 6). The scarce, expensive part is **POS, scale and cash-register integration** in retail and hospitality, plus migrating historical data from Excel and Word.
- The survey spread (7% to 35% "ready", 50% to 95% "Excel-dependent") reflects different samples and definitions. All of them point to a large unadapted base, likely more than a million businesses, that will move in a short window before the final deadline.

### Gaps
- No official count of producers' compliance declarations (declaraciones responsables) or of VeriFactu records received by the AEAT during the voluntary period was found.
- The limits of the AEAT free app (invoice volume, features) were not retrieved, because the guide is a PDF that was not read.
- No official cost estimate (MAIN) for small businesses was found.
- The exact October 2028 day and the legal instrument (RDL vs RD) are not yet known.

## 2. Mandatory B2B e-invoicing (Ley 18/2022 "Crea y Crece" art. 12; RD 238/2026; Orden HAC/1028/2026)

### Takeaway
The regulation is approved and the clock is running. RD 238/2026 of 25 March 2026 was published in the BOE on 31 March 2026 and took effect on 20 April 2026. Orden HAC/1028/2026, published on 5 October 2026 and in force from 6 October 2026, started the deadlines.
- **6 October 2027:** firms with more than €8M turnover must issue, send and receive structured B2B e-invoices and report invoice and payment status.
- **6 October 2028:** everyone else must issue, send and receive e-invoices; companies of €8M or less must also report statuses.
- **6 October 2029:** individuals (personas físicas) and entidades en atribución de rentas must report statuses.

Firms can use private platforms (which must interconnect free of charge) or the free AEAT **Solución Pública de Facturación Electrónica (SPFE)**. Anyone not issuing through the SPFE must send it a UBL "copia fiel" of every invoice. The purpose of status reporting is to monitor payment periods (morosidad).

### Cited Findings
- **RD 238/2026.** Published in the BOE on 31 March 2026, in force 20 days later. It "desarrolla el sistema de facturación electrónica obligatoria entre empresarios y profesionales" and the requirements for exchange platforms. Application counts from the ministerial order: 12 months for firms with more than €8M volumen de operaciones in the prior year (art. 121 LIVA), and 24 months for the rest. The AEAT will provide a **free** application or form. — [AEAT, 31 Mar 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/marzo/31/facturacion-electronica-obligatoria.html). One source dates Council of Ministers approval to 24 March 2026 and entry into force to 20 April 2026. — [B2Brouter, updated 18 Jun 2026](https://www.b2brouter.net/es/plazos-obligatoriedad-factura-electronica-b2b/)
- **Orden HAC/1028/2026 of 2 October.** Published in BOE núm. 247 on 5 October 2026 (BOE-A-2026-20587, pp. 129349–129390). The preamble sets out the system:
  - The obligation covers invoices to empresarios or profesionales with their seat, permanent establishment or domicile in Spain.
  - Firms not using the SPFE must send "simultáneamente a su emisión, una copia electrónica fiel de cada factura en la sintaxis UBL" to the SPFE.
  - The SPFE is "especialmente pensado para… pymes y profesionales", is free ("tendrán carácter gratuito"), acts as the universal mandatory repository, and receives payment data "con el objetivo prioritario de monitorizar los plazos de pago".
  - Its entry into force starts the deadlines.
  - Source: [BOE, Orden HAC/1028/2026 (PDF)](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/10/05/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-20587.pdf)
- **Free interoperability.** Under art. 2 bis of Ley 56/2007, private platforms must guarantee "interconexión e interoperabilidad gratuitas". Ley 7/2024 created the SPFE through DA 21ª of Ley 56/2007. — [BOE Orden HAC/1028/2026](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/10/05/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-20587.pdf)
- **Phased dates.** 06/10/2027 (>€8M, including status reporting), 06/10/2028 (all others; legal entities ≤€8M also report statuses), 06/10/2029 (personas físicas and atribución de rentas must report statuses). — [AEAT, SPFE news, 7 Oct 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/octubre/7/solucion-publica-facturacion-electronica.html). The same calendar applies to autónomos, who must issue and receive from 2028 and report statuses voluntarily until 2029. — [AyE, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/hacienda/hacienda-confirma-que-autonomos-tendran-que-usar-factura-electronica-obligatoria-proximos-meses/20261007212015056227.html)
- **Transitional rules.**
  - During the first 12 months, >€8M issuers must attach a readable PDF unless the recipient accepts the original format.
  - Public-sector subcontractors get up to 24 months.
  - Private platforms have 12 months to meet the copia fiel and interconnection requirements.
  - The SPFE must be available at least two months before first effective application.
  - Sources: [Derecho y Abogados, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.derechoabogados.es/noticias/orden-hac-1028-2026-solucion-publica-facturacion-electronica-boe/); [AyE, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/hacienda/hacienda-confirma-que-autonomos-tendran-que-usar-factura-electronica-obligatoria-proximos-meses/20261007212015056227.html)
- **Scope.** B2B only; consumer sales are excluded. Ordinary simplified invoices are excluded, but qualified simplified invoices may be caught. There is no general sector exclusion. Turnover is measured under VAT rules. — [AyE, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/hacienda/hacienda-confirma-que-autonomos-tendran-que-usar-factura-electronica-obligatoria-proximos-meses/20261007212015056227.html)
- **Status reporting.**
  - The recipient must report rejection, full payment date and due date, and may report receipt of goods or of the invoice.
  - Invoices are presumed accepted if not rejected.
  - The issuer may report collections or non-payment.
  - Source: [Derecho y Abogados, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.derechoabogados.es/noticias/orden-hac-1028-2026-solucion-publica-facturacion-electronica-boe/)
  - Reporting window of **4 calendar days** (single-source). — [B2Brouter](https://www.b2brouter.net/es/plazos-obligatoriedad-factura-electronica-b2b/)
- **Formats.** Facturae, UBL, CII and EDIFACT are accepted; platforms must handle all four. **Sanctions of up to €10,000** (single-source). — [B2Brouter](https://www.b2brouter.net/es/plazos-obligatoriedad-factura-electronica-b2b/)
- **Link to VeriFactu and TicketBAI.** The order's UBL mapping has a QR field (BT-123) whose value must be «VERIFACTU» or «TICKETBAI». — [BOE Orden HAC/1028/2026, annex](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/10/05/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-20587.pdf)
- **Lock-in.** Exchange between platforms "no garantiza por sí solo que los pequeños negocios puedan trasladar todo su historial de facturas". RD 238/2026 arts. 7–8 require format conversion and interconnection on request, but not migration of program-specific data. A CNMC analysis of cloud services (16 Sep 2026) flags data-extraction costs and portability barriers. Records must be kept 4 years (tax prescription) and 6 years (Código de Comercio art. 30). — [AyE, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/factura-electronica-pone-alerta-autonomos-control-datos/20261015000100056311.html)
- **ATA position.** VeriFactu and e-invoicing should not be "procesos independientes". Lorenzo Amor: "la falta de coordinación puede obligar a los autónomos a realizar inversiones sucesivas". — [AyE, 17 Sep 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/info-ata/autonomos-tendrian-que-adaptarse-verifactu-meses-ata-pide-que-sea-vez-que-factura-electronica/20260916172435055901.html). AEDAF backed a single roadmap (e-invoicing, VeriFactu, SII, ViDA). — [AyE, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/hacienda/hacienda-cede-autonomos-tendran-ano-mas-adaptar-programas-facturacion-verifactu/20261005152934056163.html)
- **Network pricing benchmark (B2Brouter, + IVA).** Basic €0 (1 user, 24 transactions/year, VeriFactu and Peppol send). Professional **€110/year**. Business **€300/year**. — [B2Brouter precios, 2026](https://www.b2brouter.net/es/precios/)

### Inferences
- B2B e-invoicing is a bigger behavioural change than VeriFactu. Every supplier-customer pair needs connectivity, accounts payable must report rejection and payment dates, and the state will see real payment periods. That ties tax compliance to late-payment enforcement (overlap with the cash-flow/morosidad researcher).
- The free SPFE puts a price floor of zero on basic issuing and receiving. Paid value will sit in AP/AR automation (matching, payment-date reporting fed from bank data), ERP and POS integration, multi-format conversion, archives that survive switching vendors, and asesoría-managed multi-client flows.
- The >€8M firms (12 months, with a PDF allowance) will push suppliers to adopt before their own 2028 date, so SME demand will start in 2027.

### Gaps
- No official count of firms above €8M, and no official compliance-cost estimate (MAIN), was found.
- The SPFE's go-live date is not yet known. It must be available two months before 6 Oct 2027.
- The sanctions regime was not verified in an official text; the €10,000 figure is single-source.

## 3. Regional precedents: TicketBAI and Batuz (Basque Country), Navarra, Canarias (IGIC)

### Takeaway
The Basque Country (Bilbao, Vitoria-Gasteiz, Donostia-San Sebastián) already runs real-time invoice reporting through TicketBAI. Bizkaia adds Batuz/LROE: online record books and draft returns prepared by the Hacienda Foral. Roll-out took four years (2022 to 2026), was phased by sector with retail, hospitality and personal services among the last, and needed:
- tax deductions of 30%, or 60% for early adopters, on adaptation costs;
- age-based exemptions for older sole traders;
- partial exemptions for vending machines and estancos;
- free official invoicing apps.

The Diputación's own worked examples put adaptation spend at €1,200–€2,500 per business. TicketBAI is unaffected by the VeriFactu postponement. Navarra's regime could not be verified. Canarias runs a separate IGIC administration (ATC) with its own SII-IGIC, and some online asesorías do not serve it.

### Cited Findings
- **Bizkaia (Batuz) final calendar, Norma Foral 8/2023.**
  - 01/01/2024: large IS taxpayers.
  - 01/07/2024: individuals and micro/SMEs in industry, construction, transport and most professional activities.
  - 01/01/2025: wholesale; and micro/SME companies in retail, hospitality, repairs and hairdressing.
  - 01/07/2025: individuals in retail, hospitality, repairs, hairdressing and other personal services.
  - 01/01/2026: exempt entities, non-profits, agriculture, fishing, education and cultural services.

- **Navarra.** Not verified. navarra.es/es/hacienda returned HTTP 503 on three attempts. One commercial blog says Navarra "has its own system (Batuz)". - **Canarias.** The Agencia Tributaria Canaria runs a separate "SII del IGIC" service. — [ATC home](https://www3.gobiernodecanarias.org/tributos/atc/). ### Inferences
- The Basque experience suggests the hardest segment is small retail, hospitality and personal services run by older owners. They were phased last, and exemptions were needed for owners near retirement and for estancos and vending. Expect the same pattern in common territory with VeriFactu.
- Administrations supplied free basic apps in every Basque territory, so vendors competed on POS integration, multi-device setups and support. VeriFactu (with the free AEAT app) and the SPFE copy the same model.
- Firms operating in both Basque and common territory will need TicketBAI, VeriFactu, the B2B e-invoice and the SII simultaneously. The UBL QR field already admits both «VERIFACTU» and «TICKETBAI».

### Gaps
- No Basque press evidence was retrieved on bar or shop closures, protests or adoption statistics in Bilbao, Vitoria or Donostia. The search budget was exhausted before this could be covered.
- No figures on the number of TicketBAI devices or invoices.
- Navarra's regime and the Canarias IGIC small-business threshold (franquicia) were not verified.
- Ceuta and Melilla (IPSI) were not researched.

## 4. Autónomos: real-income contributions, regularisation, filings, penalties, notifications and embargos, inspections, módulos

### Takeaway
About **3.41 million** people are in the RETA (Feb 2026). They face:
- A contribution system based on real income with 15 tramos (**€205–€1,606/month** in 2026). The tables were frozen at 2025 levels for 2026, and the MEI rises to 1% in 2027.
- An annual regularisation that 35% of surveyed autónomos ended up paying. They rate how it was handled at **2.2/10**.
- A **~40% minimum-base increase** in 2026 for societarios and family collaborators, to be collected later through regularisation.
- Four quarterly filing rounds plus annual returns. ATA estimates **226 hours and about €3,390 per year** spent on bureaucracy.
- Fixed fines and surcharges for small slips, and faster enforcement: monthly card and Bizum reporting by banks, and bank embargos executed within about 24 hours.
- Módulos taxpayers had two decrees extending their limits fail in Congress (RDL 16/2025 and RDL 2/2026). The AEAT kept the old limits for 2026 by interpretation; 2027 is again pending.

### Inferences
- The contribution system turned a fixed monthly cost into a forecast that is settled later. Autónomos who cannot predict income, and societarios whose base changed by order, face lump-sum bills one or two years later. There is room for continuous income-versus-tramo tracking and "regularisation exposure" alerts.
- Enforcement is getting more automated (monthly modelo 170, 24-hour embargos, electronic notifications deemed delivered), while taxpayers' own processes are still manual. The mismatch shows up as letters, fines and frozen accounts for formal or reconciliation errors rather than fraud.
- Módulos users (bars, transport, small retail, farmers) carry the most legal uncertainty: three decrees were not convalidated in 2025–2026 and the 2027 limits are pending again. They are also less digitised, since TaxDown excludes them and they are least likely to use software.

### Gaps
- No official Seguridad Social totals for the 2023 and 2024 regularisations (how many paid, how many were refunded, € amounts) were retrieved.
- AEAT counts of comprobaciones and letters to small businesses were not found; the Plan Anual de Control Tributario 2026 was not retrieved.
- The total number of autónomos in módulos is unknown (only the roughly 800,000 farmers).
- Penalties for modelos 347 and 349 specifically were not checked beyond art. 198.

## 5. The gestoría/asesoría market: prices, satisfaction, complaints, online asesorías

### Takeaway
Online asesorías have set a public price band:
- **€25–€100/month + IVA** for autónomos (Infoautónomos from €24.95; TaxDown €29.90–€59.90; Declarando €49.90–€99.90 with taxes included; Cuéntica Fiscalidad Tutelada €59);

## 7. Other recurring tax friction (VAT cash flow, SII, accounts deposit, new VAT conditions, certificates, crypto)

### Takeaway
Several frictions recur:
- VAT is owed on invoices not yet collected, and recovering it requires formal modification of the taxable base. A TEAC ruling (22 Sep 2026) eased one trap, the late sending of corrective invoices.
- Quarterly filers can only reclaim VAT credits in the Q4 return (January). Foreign VAT refunds take at least four months, and domestic refunds can be embargoed.
- Micro-SLs face accounts-deposit fines of €1,200–€60,000, plus a registry block.
- From 1 December 2026, the 10% VAT rate on home renovations requires traceable payment (RDL 29/2026, which is subject to convalidation in Congress).

Public-contract tax certificates and crypto or foreign-income reporting were not researched in depth and look marginal for SMEs.

### Cited Findings
- **VAT on unpaid invoices.** TEAC RG 00/07774/2024 (22 Sep 2026): sending the corrective invoice (rectificativa) late to an insolvent client does not by itself bar VAT recovery, provided the base modification was communicated to the AEAT on time and no harm or fraud is shown. REAF: "Hacienda no puede rechazar la recuperación del IVA por el mero retraso". This is not yet binding doctrine. — [AyE, 10 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/hacienda/muchos-autonomos-podran-recuperar-iva-facturas-rectificativas-hayan-enviado-tarde/20261009173758056270.html)
- **Refund timing.**
  - Quarterly filers with a VAT credit can request a refund only in the Q4 return (1–30 Jan 2027).
  - Foreign EU VAT (modelo 360) had to be claimed by 30 Sep 2026, and the refunding state generally has **four months** to reply.
  - Source: [AyE, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/hacienda/autonomos-tienen-solo-cinco-dias-cobrar-iva-ventas-que-hicieron-fuera-espana/20260925172623056019.html)
- **SII and REDEME.** An extraordinary window to renounce SII bookkeeping and leave REDEME ran until 16 Feb 2026 under the failed decrees. — [AEAT, 9 Feb 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/febrero/9/medidas-materia-tributacion.html); [AEAT April 2026 news list](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/abril.html). SII users are excluded from VeriFactu. — [BOE RD 1007/2023](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-24840). The AEAT SII page (updated 14 Oct 2026) cites RD 596/2016 and Orden HFP/417/2017. — [AEAT SII](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/suministro-inmediato-informacion.html)
- **Annual accounts (Ley de Sociedades de Capital).**
  - Art. 279: deposit within a month of approval.
  - Art. 282: registry block ("cierre registral") while non-compliance persists.
  - Art. 283: ICAC fine of "1.200 a 60.000 euros", up to €300,000 per year of delay if turnover exceeds €6M; halved if filed before proceedings start; prescribes in three years.
  - Source: [BOE, LSC consolidated](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2010-10544)
- **New VAT condition for renovations.**
  - RDL 29/2026 (6 Oct 2026; BOE 7 Oct 2026, BOE-A-2026-20823) is a housing decree whose Title II "introduce importantes novedades" in IRPF and VAT. — [AEAT, 7 Oct 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/octubre/7/medidas-fiscales-tributarias-introducidas-real-292026.html)
  - Per the press, from **1 Dec 2026** the 10% rate on renovations applies only if payment is by card, transfer, nominative cheque or bank deposit; cash means 21%. It also extends 10% to renovations of rented habitual homes. The 40% materials test and the 2-year dwelling-age test still apply. — [AyE, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/autonomos-pymes-reformas-podran-aplicar-iva-10-cobran-efectivo/20261016000200056348.html) (single press source for the details)
  - An earlier housing decree, RDL 26/2026, was **not convalidated** (Congress resolution of 2 Oct 2026). — [AEAT, 30 Sep 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/septiembre/30/medidas-fiscales-tributarias-introducidas-real-262026.html)
- **Expense deductibility** is a recurring source of disputes (see Section 4). — [AyE, 20 Oct 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/hacienda/hacienda-admite-que-autonomos-tienen-demasiadas-trabas-deducir-coche-movil-comidas-suministros/20261019135900056391.html)

### Inferences
- RDL 29/2026 shows that VAT rate determination increasingly depends on facts that invoicing tools do not capture (payment method, materials share, dwelling age, rental use). Rule-aware invoicing for trades is a niche opportunity (overlap with the construction researcher).
- Repeated decree failures (the 2025 omnibus decree, RDL 16/2025, RDL 2/2026, RDL 26/2026) make "is this rule actually in force?" a real, recurring question for SMEs and asesorías.

## Pain-point inventory (structured entries)

### TAX-01: VeriFactu forces adaptation or replacement of invoicing software, TPVs, cash registers and scales
- **Who:** All sectors that invoice with software. Autónomos, micro (<10), small (10–49) and medium (50–249) firms, and startups. Hardest hit: retail with weighing scales (fruterías, carnicerías), bars and restaurants with TPVs, personal services. Excluded: SII users (generally larger firms; SII thresholds not verified here) and Basque foral taxpayers.
- **Where:** National (territorio común): Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Sevilla, Zaragoza, Málaga, Palma, Murcia, Alicante, Valladolid, Vigo, A Coruña. It very likely also applies in Las Palmas and Santa Cruz, since IRPF and IS are state taxes in Canarias (inference, not verified). Not in Bilbao, Vitoria or Donostia (TicketBAI). Navarra is unverified.
- **Day-to-day reality:** Owners must find out whether their invoicing program, TPV or scale counts as a "SIF". They then need the vendor's adapted version (hash chaining, QR, event log, or VERI*FACTU submission), and in POS settings often new hardware. Tickets count as simplified invoices, so the shop till is in scope.
- **Evidence:**
  - The legal compliance dates are 1 Jan 2027 (IS) and 1 Jul 2027 (rest). — [BOE consolidated RD 1007/2023, update 03/12/2025](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-24840); [AEAT FAQ, 21 Oct 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu/preguntas-frecuentes.html)

### TAX-02: Regulatory whiplash and "compliance limbo" around VeriFactu dates

- **Scale:** Same as TAX-01, about 4.6M taxpayers (single-source estimate).
- **Cost/impact:** Sunk adaptation spend; repeated advisory communications; planning paralysis. No € figure available.

- **Confidence:** High.

### TAX-03: Excel/Word/manual invoicing in a legal grey zone and forced migration
- **Who:** Autónomos and micro firms (professionals, trades, freelancers), and asesorías that issue invoices for clients.
- **Where:** National (territorio común).
- **Day-to-day reality:** Many still produce invoices from Word or Excel templates. The DGT says this is "no automáticamente excluido": if Excel calculates, stores or generates invoices it is treated as a SIF. Handwritten paper invoices are outside the rule. Migrating means moving client lists, numbering series and history into a compliant tool.
- **Evidence:**

### TAX-05: Mandatory B2B e-invoicing (structured formats, platforms, UBL copy to the AEAT)
- **Who:** Every business and professional invoicing other businesses in Spain. Firms above €8M turnover go first; then SMEs, autónomos and startups. B2C sellers are mostly unaffected.
- **Where:** National. The UBL mapping accepts TicketBAI QR references, which implies Basque firms are covered (inference).
- **Day-to-day reality:** From Oct 2027 for large firms and Oct 2028 for everyone else, an emailed PDF stops being a valid B2B invoice. Firms must issue and receive structured e-invoices via a private platform or the free SPFE. Anyone not using the SPFE must send it a UBL copy at the moment of issue. Large customers will demand e-invoices from suppliers from 2027.
- **Evidence:**
  - RD 238/2026 (BOE 31 Mar 2026). — [AEAT, 31 Mar 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/marzo/31/facturacion-electronica-obligatoria.html)

  - Dates 6 Oct 2027, 6 Oct 2028 and 6 Oct 2029. — [AEAT, 7 Oct 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/octubre/7/solucion-publica-facturacion-electronica.html)

Integration with ERP and POS is not quantified. ATA fears a second investment round after VeriFactu.

  - Legal basis: Ley 18/2022 art. 12 (art. 2 bis Ley 56/2007), Ley 7/2024 (DA 21ª Ley 56/2007), RD 238/2026 (in force 20 Apr 2026), Orden HAC/1028/2026 (in force 6 Oct 2026).
  - Status: **upcoming deadlines** of 6 Oct 2027 (>€8M), 6 Oct 2028 (rest) and 6 Oct 2029 (status reporting for personas físicas).
- **How it is solved today:** B2G experience via FACe and Facturae; invoicing SaaS with Facturae and UBL output (TS Facturas, Contasimple Profesional with FACe, Anfix); networks such as B2Brouter and Peppol; the coming free SPFE.
- **Software-solvable?:** Yes.
- **Confidence:** High on the rules; low on cost estimates.

### TAX-06: Invoice-status and payment-date reporting (rejection, due date, full payment)

- **Driver & status:** Constitutional tax regimes (foral) and the REF of Canarias. In force.
- **How it is solved today:** Local gestorías; foral free apps (FakturaBAI, FakturAraba, Haz tu factura); vendors with TicketBAI certification (TS Facturas).
- **Software-solvable?:** Yes. Localised filing and invoicing engines (IGIC, TicketBAI/LROE, IPSI).
- **Confidence:** Medium-low. The evidence is one provider's exclusions plus official regime facts.

## Top pains
Ranked by severity times breadth, using evidence quality as a tiebreaker:
1. **TAX-05 and TAX-06: B2B e-invoicing plus status and payment reporting.** It touches every B2B invoicer, with fixed legal dates (6 Oct 2027, 2028, 2029) now running under Orden HAC/1028/2026. It changes invoicing and AP processes, and the free SPFE moves paid value to integration and AP/AR automation.
2. **TAX-01 and TAX-02: VeriFactu adaptation under moving deadlines.** About 4.6M taxpayers (single-source estimate) and only 7–35% ready. POS and scale hardware costs €1,800–€2,500+. The legal 2027 dates coexist with an announced but unpublished October 2028 date.
3. **TAX-12 and TAX-13: Real-income cuota and regularisation shocks.** 3.41M autónomos; 35% of surveyed autónomos paid after regularisation; handling rated 2.2/10. Societarios and collaborators face +40% bases and lump-sum back-charges in 2027–28.
4. **TAX-09 and TAX-10: The filing treadmill and fixed penalties.** Four filing rounds a year plus annual returns. 226 hours (€3,390) a year of bureaucracy per ATA, and 42% want fewer filings. Automatic surcharges and €100–€600 fines for formal slips.
5. **TAX-15, TAX-18 and TAX-19: Automated enforcement against manual businesses.** Monthly card and Bizum reporting since January 2026, notification traps, and 24-hour bank embargos. Small B2C firms pay for reconciliation failures rather than fraud.
6. **TAX-17: Deductible-expense uncertainty.** Hacienda's own Consejo para la Defensa del Contribuyente calls the rules "excesivamente restrictivas". This is the most common trigger of disputes and letters for professionals.
7. **TAX-14: Módulos legal uncertainty.** Narrower (hospitality, transport, retail, farmers) but acute. Decrees failed in Congress twice in 2026 and the 2027 limits are pending again.
8. **TAX-22 and TAX-23: Adviser dependence and the asesoría productivity gap.** Paid help (€25–€130/month) still produces errors and penalties. Small, Excel-heavy asesorías are the bottleneck and the distribution channel for any fix.

## Noise and dead ends
- **VeriFactu dates of 1 Jan 2026 and 1 Jul 2026** are obsolete, moved by RDL 15/2025 (BOE consolidated text). Any source citing 2026 deadlines (for example Merca2, Jul 2025) is out of date.
- **"VeriFactu has been postponed to 2028"** is not yet law. It is an official announcement (5 Oct 2026) without a BOE norm; the AEAT FAQ of 21 Oct 2026 still lists 2027. Treat it as probable, not certain, given that three 2026 decrees were not convalidated.
- **Infoautónomos' claim that >€8M firms face VeriFactu in October 2027** is not supported by the Hacienda note. It likely conflates the e-invoicing date.
- **"VeriFactu forces everyone to buy software"** is false. Handwritten paper invoicing is outside the regulation, and free compliant options exist (AEAT app, FacturaScripts, Contasimple free tier). The pure invoicing-software opportunity at the low end is commoditised.
- **B2B e-invoicing as "already mandatory"** is false. Nothing is enforceable before 6 Oct 2027, so headlines about immediate obligations are noise.
- **TicketBAI as a current pain** is mostly solved. Calendars completed by 1 Jan 2026, deductions have ended, and free apps and certified vendors exist. Remaining friction is technical churn for vendors. There is no evidence of ongoing small-business distress (none found, but the search was limited).
- **The VAT franchise (IVA franquiciado)** is a real demand but not software-solvable. It is a policy decision, and all evidence comes from ATA (single-source chain).
- **ATA's aggregate cost figures** (more than €10,000M or €11,000M a year; 226 hours) are advocacy numbers with undisclosed methods. Use them as indicative only.
- **Readiness percentages** (7%, 35%, 65%, 88%, 95% Excel) come from vendor surveys with different samples and conflicts of interest (TeamSystem and Quipu are the same group). Do not average them.
- **Odoo pricing** was displayed in USD by geolocation and is unreliable for Spain.
- **Xolo in Spain**: the Spanish page returned 404 and Spanish prices and operations are unverified.
- **Navarra's own invoicing regime**: unverified (official site down), and one commercial source is demonstrably wrong (it calls Batuz Navarra's).
- **Crypto/DAC8, foreign-income reporting and public-contract tax certificates** were not evidenced as SME pain points within this scope. The certificate is an online AEAT download, and procurement is covered by another researcher.
- **Regional "cuota cero" schemes** are subsidies (another researcher's scope). They reduce, not create, burden, and have budget-limited windows.
- **Startups (Ley 28/2022) tax incentives** were not researched here; covered by the startups researcher. No evidence gathered on their tax-compliance friction.
