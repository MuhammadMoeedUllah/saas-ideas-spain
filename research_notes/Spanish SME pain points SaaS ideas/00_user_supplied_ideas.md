# User-supplied ideas to validate

Added by the user on 2026-10-23 as part of the research plan. These are **hypotheses, not
verified findings**. The claims below are reproduced as given and must be checked against
evidence before any idea is kept, reshaped or discarded.

Framing given by the user: to build a successful software or gaming product for Spain's major
cities, target local bureaucratic friction, the mid-term housing crisis for remote workers, or
community-driven, streamer-integrated social gaming. Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia and Málaga are
said to have distinct operational pain points and digital behaviours.

## SaaS ideas

### U1. ColivEs: mid-term compliance and housing management SaaS
- Target cities: Barcelona, Madrid, Málaga
- Claimed need: severe housing shortages; strict municipal crackdowns on short-term holiday
  licences; a constant influx of high-earning digital nomads and remote corporate workers
- Idea: a B2B dashboard that helps landlords turn traditional or tourist apartments into
  flexible coliving hubs
- Features: automate local rent caps and regional legal compliance; integrate European digital
  ID (eIDAS) identity verification; dynamic pricing based on digital-nomad moving patterns

### U2. PermisoFast: AI bureaucracy co-pilot for expats and scaleups
- Target cities: Madrid, Barcelona, Málaga
- Claimed need: NIE numbers, cita previa bookings and Beckham Law tax status suffer heavy,
  frustrating backlogs
- Idea: a generative-AI suite for corporate HR teams and relocation agencies
- Features: track real-time changes on Spanish regional government portals; pre-fill complex
  forms by extracting data from documents; use computer vision to catch application errors
  before final submission

### U3. EcoRoute: low-emission-zone compliance engine
- Target cities: Valencia, Madrid, Barcelona
- Claimed need: strict low-emission zones enforced by automated camera tracking put a heavy
  logistical burden on commercial fleets and local e-commerce delivery partners
- Idea: a green routing API built on municipal open data
- Features: connect to public open-data feeds in Barcelona and Valencia; optimise daily
  delivery routes to avoid emission penalties; use Valencia's "Urban Sandbox" legal framework
  to test new smart-mobility models

## Game and gaming-platform ideas

### U4. Fandango Arena: streamer-interactive party game
- Target market: national
- Claimed trend: a large player shift away from competitive shooters toward premium, comedic
  co-op games with proximity voice chat; audiences strongly shaped by streamers such as
  Ibai Llanos and AuronPlay
- Idea: a mid-priced (~$30) multiplayer party game built around funny physics failures
- Features: native Twitch and Kick API integration; viewers vote on rule changes in real time;
  custom proximity voice chat to produce shareable clips

### U5. LigaHub: creator tournament and fantasy engine
- Target market: national sports entertainment
- Claimed trend: the success of the Kings League shows Spanish audiences love streamer-led,
  gamified sports; rigid traditional esports formats are declining in engagement
- Idea: an automated white-label platform for mid-tier streamers to build and broadcast their
  own custom gaming leagues
- Features: draft systems for community-sourced players; viewer prediction systems and dynamic
  leaderboards; transfer-market tools "mirroring real money transactions"
