# Mid-term rentals (alquiler de temporada), coliving and remote-worker housing in Spain: pain points, regulation, disputes, market size and tools (status as of 23 October 2026)

Scope: Barcelona, Madrid, Málaga and Valencia first, then Palma, Alicante, Sevilla, Las Palmas de Gran Canaria and Santa Cruz de Tenerife. Seen from both sides: landlords, property managers and coliving operators, and the remote workers, nomads, students and relocating employees who rent. Overlaps with the hospitality (tourist rentals), real-estate (long-term landlords) and expat (visas/NIE) tracks are mentioned only briefly.

Method note: national law was checked against the BOE text of Real Decreto-ley 29/2026 (downloaded and searched verbatim). The shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through, so later checks used direct fetches of URLs already found. This left some regional items (Valencia, Sevilla, Alicante, Santa Cruz) and some tool prices unverified. They are flagged below. "Search-level" means the figure appeared in a search snippet and the page itself could not be opened.

## 1. Regulation of seasonal / mid-term rentals as of October 2026 (national, regional, city, courts, fines)

### Takeaway
On 8 Oct 2026 Spain turned the "alquiler de temporada" into an "arrendamiento de vivienda temporal" (Real Decreto-ley 29/2026). It now needs a written, provable cause of temporary displacement, must last more than 31 days and normally no more than 12 months, and is covered by caps on rent, guarantees and fees. The decree's survival is uncertain: a near-identical decree (RDL 26/2026) was voted down on 2 Oct, the Cortes were dissolved on 5 Oct, and RDL 29/2026 awaits a Diputación Permanente vote expected around 12 Nov 2026, ahead of elections on 29 Nov. Catalonia has applied its own, stricter version since January 2026 (Llei 11/2025). The national Registro Único under RD 1312/2024 was struck down by the Supreme Court in May–June 2026.

### Cited Findings

**National: RDL 29/2026 (verbatim from BOE)**
- RDL 29/2026, de 6 de octubre, "por el que se adoptan medidas urgentes para la protección de la función social de la vivienda y la ampliación de la oferta de vivienda asequible", was published in BOE núm. 249 on 7 Oct 2026 (BOE-A-2026-20823) and entered into force the next day. It amends the LAU (art. 3), Ley 12/2023 (art. 4), the LEC, IRPF and IVA. — [BOE, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823); [ICAB, Oct 2026](https://icab.es/es/actualidad/noticias/noticia/Real-Decreto-ley-29-2026-de-6-de-octubre-por-el-que-se-adoptan-medidas-urgentes-para-la-proteccion-de-la-funcion-social-de-la-vivienda-y-la-ampliacion-de-la-oferta-de-vivienda-asequible/); [AEAT note, 7 Oct 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/en_gb/todas-noticias/2026/octubre/7/medidas-fiscales-tributarias-introducidas-real-292026.html)
- New art. 2.3 LAU: "Se entenderá por arrendamiento de vivienda temporal aquel arrendamiento que recae sobre una edificación habitable, incluido el arrendamiento parcial de una habitación o estancia, que venga a cubrir la necesidad de vivienda del arrendatario cuando éste se encuentra temporalmente desplazado de su domicilio habitual, por causa justificada y acreditable." (A temporary housing lease covers the need of a tenant temporarily displaced from their habitual home for a justified, provable cause, including a single room.) New art. 2.1 brings room rentals inside "vivienda habitual". — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 3.2 LAU: commercial-use leases are "en todo caso excluidos los que tengan por objeto el alojamiento u hospedaje de personas". Housing people can no longer be contracted as "uso distinto de vivienda", which was the old art. 3 route for seasonal lets. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 5.e LAU: tourist lets stay outside the LAU, but their "duración máxima ... no podrá en ningún caso superar los treinta y un días". New art. 5.f excludes rooms ceded by non-profit third-sector programmes. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 7.2 LAU: the contract must "prever de forma expresa la causa que motiva el desplazamiento temporal". The cause must be "real y acreditable, recayendo sobre el arrendador la carga de la prueba". Without it the lease is treated as habitual housing "con efectos retroactivos a la formalización del contrato". — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 9 bis: duration "superior a treinta y un días" and, as a general rule, no more than 12 months in total. If it exceeds 12 months without justification, or if "más de dos contratos" are chained between the same parties on the same dwelling, "el primero se entenderá celebrado como un contrato de arrendamiento de vivienda habitual". The 5-year minimum (7 if the landlord is a company) then applies. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 11.2: the temporary tenant may leave after one month with 10 days' notice and owes no compensation. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 17.8 (nationwide, not only in tensioned zones): "Cuando en una misma vivienda se celebren de forma sucesiva varios contratos de arrendamiento de vivienda temporal, la renta correspondiente a los posteriores no podrá suponer un incremento porcentual anual superior al ... (IRAV) vigente." New art. 17.9: the sum of simultaneous room rents "no podrá exceder el importe de la renta del contrato ... de la vivienda completa". In tensioned zones the art. 17.6–17.7 caps also apply. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 20.2: management and formalisation costs "no podrán ser repercutidos al arrendatario, ni directa ni indirectamente, bajo ningún concepto o denominación". Other non-essential services can be charged only if the tenant asks for them in writing after being told they are optional and what they cost. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 21 bis: in temporary housing the landlord must answer repair requests within 5 working days. Otherwise the tenant may do the repair and deduct it from next month's rent. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New art. 36.5: "En ningún caso podrá exigirse al arrendatario la contratación de seguros de impago de renta u otras coberturas análogas". The additional guarantee is capped at 2 months for habitual leases and "una mensualidad" for temporary leases. New art. 36.7 requires a signed end-of-lease condition document. If none is signed, or it lists no damage, the dwelling is presumed returned in good condition. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- Transitional rule (new DT 8ª LAU): seasonal contracts signed before RDL 29/2026 keep the "uso distinto" regime until their agreed end date and then expire "sin posibilidad de prórroga". Renewals fall under the new regime. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- New DA 12ª LAU: regional civil and housing law is preserved, with Catalonia named explicitly. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- Platform sanctions (new Title V LAU, tied to the Ventanilla Única Digital de Arrendamientos and Reg. (EU) 2024/1028):
  - very serious: not collecting and transmitting data monthly or quarterly;
  - serious: incomplete or inaccurate data;
  - minor: not interconnecting with the Ventanilla, or transmitting late. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
  - Reported fine levels for the same scheme in RDL 26/2026: up to €1M or 2% of global turnover (very serious), €500,000 (serious), €100,000 (minor). — [idealista, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/09/30/916608-el-gobierno-obliga-a-justificar-cada-alquiler-de-temporada-y-limita-el-de)
- Tax changes:
  - From 1 Dec 2026, furnished flats let for ≤30 nights to the same tenant, other than the landlord's own habitual home, lose the VAT exemption and pay 10%. Lets with hotel-type services remain taxable.
  - Municipalities in tensioned zones may add an IBI surcharge of up to 50% on residential property used for tourist accommodation, 100% for owners of 2 or more such properties and 150% for 4 or more. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823); [idealista, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/09/30/916608-el-gobierno-obliga-a-justificar-cada-alquiler-de-temporada-y-limita-el-de)
- IRPF: the rewritten art. 23.2 LIRPF applies the rental-income reduction to "arrendamiento de bienes inmuebles destinados a vivienda a que se refiere el apartado 1 del artículo 2 de la LAU", i.e. habitual housing. Temporary leases fall under art. 2.3. — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823) (see Inferences)
- Evictions: the decree extends a suspension regime for vulnerable households "hasta el 31 de diciembre de 2030" and adds an "enervación extraordinaria" step. It also cross-references RDL 8/2026 of 20 Mar 2026, "de medidas en el alquiler en respuesta a las consecuencias económicas y sociales de la Guerra de Irán". — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)

**Catalonia / Barcelona**
- The Parlament approved the law regulating seasonal and room rentals on 18 Dec 2025. PSC, ERC, Comuns and CUP voted for it. Junts opposed the seasonal and room provisions, and PP, Vox and AC voted against. The PP announced an appeal to the TC. — [Parlament de Catalunya, Dec 2025](https://www.parlament.cat/web/actualitat/noticies/index.html?p_id=326136273); [idealista/ca, 18 Dec 2025](https://www.idealista.com/ca/news/immobiliari/vivienda/2025/12/18/877252-catalunya-aprova-limitar-el-preu-del-lloguer-de-temporada-i-d-habitacions); [Ara, Dec 2025](https://en.ara.cat/economy/green-light-for-the-law-to-regulate-seasonal-rentals-in-catalonia_1_5595980.html)
- The law is Llei 11/2025, del 29 de desembre, de mesures en matèria d'habitatge i urbanisme. It was published on the Wednesday before New Year's Day (31 Dec 2025), and idealista/ca reported entry into force on 2 Jan 2026. Other sources say it applies to contracts from 1 Jan 2026.
  - Caps apply to seasonal and room rentals in tensioned zones. "Només queden fora ... els contractes de lloguer temporals amb fins turístics o de vacances."
  - Room rents must sum to no more than the flat's rent.
  - Every lease is presumed habitual unless the landlord "acrediti documentalment la causa concreta de la temporalitat". — [idealista/ca, 15 Jun 2026](https://www.idealista.com/ca/news/immobiliari/2026/06/15/901949-el-constitucional-admet-el-recurs-del-pp-contra-la-regulacio-del-lloguer-temporal-i-les); [idealista, 4 Feb 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/02/04/881584-todo-sobre-la-ley-que-regula-el-alquiler-de-temporada-en-cataluna-y-lo-que-esta-por)
- How the cap works in practice, per lawyer Alejandro Fuentes-Lojo:
  - the contract, the fianza and the supporting documents are filed with INCASÒL;
  - extensions must show the cause persists;
  - prior seasonal rents do not count as the "last rent", which pushes landlords onto the SERPAVI index;
  - the index has no values for flats under 30 m² or over 150 m²;
  - only luxury homes over 300 m² and high-income tenants are excluded;
  - he argues formalisation fees can still be charged under the Catalan text. — [idealista, 4 Feb 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/02/04/881584-todo-sobre-la-ley-que-regula-el-alquiler-de-temporada-en-cataluna-y-lo-que-esta-por)
- On 15 Jun 2026 the Constitutional Court admitted the PP deputies' appeal against arts. 2.2, 3.8, 5.5, 5.6, 5.8, 5.9, 5.14, 5.15 and 8.1. No suspension is reported, so the law stays in force. — [idealista/ca, 15 Jun 2026](https://www.idealista.com/ca/news/immobiliari/2026/06/15/901949-el-constitucional-admet-el-recurs-del-pp-contra-la-regulacio-del-lloguer-temporal-i-les)
- A similar Catalan decree-law of April 2024 was rejected in the Diputació Permanent. — [Ara, Dec 2025](https://en.ara.cat/economy/green-light-for-the-law-to-regulate-seasonal-rentals-in-catalonia_1_5595980.html)
- Barcelona City Council approved a modification of the Pla General Metropolità on 27 Mar 2026 (PSC, BComú and ERC for; all other groups against).
  - Habitual and permanent residence becomes the priority use. Seasonal rentals, tourist apartments, economic uses and second homes become "assimilated uses".
  - It gives a legal basis for local rules against seasonal rentals and coliving.
  - It still needs approval from the Generalitat's urban-planning subcommittee and then a special plan or ordinance. — [Ara, 27 Mar 2026](https://en.ara.cat/society/barcelona-decrees-that-the-residential-use-of-apartments-prevails-over-temporary-use_1_5691565.html)
- Barcelona will not renew its 10,101 tourist-flat (HUT) licences on 24 Nov 2028 — [Chekin blog, 2026](https://chekin.com/blog/regulacion-pisos-turisticos-barcelona/). The TC upheld the underlying Catalan decree in 2025. — [Moncloa.com, 12 Jun 2026](https://www.moncloa.com/2026/06/12/barcelona-collboni-pisos-3384249/)

### Inferences
- Applicable rules now depend on signature date and region. Catalonia since Jan 2026: Llei 11/2025 plus case law. Rest of Spain: pre-8 Oct 2026 seasonal contracts stay "uso distinto" until expiry; contracts from 8 Oct follow RDL 29/2026 for as long as it survives. If convalidation fails around 12 Nov, the national regime reverts to the old LAU, but Catalonia's law and court re-classification under art. 6.4 CC remain. A compliance product must therefore version rules by contract date and region.
- Art. 17.8 reads literally as nationwide: rents on successive temporary contracts on the same dwelling cannot rise faster than the IRAV each year. That would neutralise dynamic repricing of temporary leases, including in non-tensioned Madrid, Málaga and Valencia. The temporary-lease route into housing (art. 2.3) also appears to be excluded from the IRPF rent reduction, which applies only to art. 2.1 leases. Both points are readings of the BOE text; no official interpretation has been seen.
- Among the nine target cities, only Barcelona is in a tensioned zone according to idealista's lists. Rent-cap compliance is therefore mainly a Barcelona/Catalonia pain, plus other tensioned areas outside scope (Basque Country, Navarra, A Coruña).

### Gaps
- No official text was seen on the convalidation vote date, and the IRAV value in force in October 2026 was not verified. The only figure seen was 2.2% for 2025, the first IRAV ([The Corner](https://thecorner.eu/?p=118291), single source).

- The vote count on RDL 26/2026 and the outcome of PL 122/000136 each rest on a single source.
- Final approval and BOCM publication of Madrid's 2023 coliving rules, and the exact date Plan Reside entered into force, were not verified.

## 2. Coliving: legal status and licensing, municipal rules, operators, occupancy and prices; conversion of tourist flats

### Takeaway
Coliving has no national legal category. Madrid created a planning class, "residencia compartida", in 2023. Barcelona regulates coliving through planning-permit enforcement, the 30% protected-housing rule and, since 2026, Catalan caps on room rents. Nationally, RDL 29/2026 makes room rentals LAU housing leases with a cap on the sum of room rents. Supply is about 19,000 operating "flex living" beds (JLL), roughly 60% of them in Madrid. Conversions of tourist flats into mid-term housing are visible in Madrid (seasonal offer +52% y/y), but Catalonia's law has shrunk Barcelona's seasonal offer by 37%.

- The obstacles to converting tourist flats are now mostly legal, not physical:
  - the cause requirement and the 12-month cap;
  - in Catalonia, room caps and index caps;
  - the 31-day ceiling on tourist lets;
  - the VAT cliff at 30 nights;
  - in Barcelona, the pending PGM change that treats seasonal use as an "assimilated" use.
- Barcelona's tourist-to-seasonal escape route is largely closed. Madrid and Málaga stay open as long as tenants have a genuine temporary cause.

    - Palma: an arrest in April 2026 for charging up to €1,100 for other people's flats — [mallorcadiario, Apr 2026](https://www.mallorcadiario.com/detenido-estafas-alquileres-palma-abril-2026) (headline-level).
- Cause documentation for remote workers and nomads (Canarias guide): "proof of domicile and residence outside Spain". The cause is weak "if it reflects only personal choice, or if the person is moving their life here". — [Lázaro Amable, 22 Oct 2026](https://lazaroamable.com/blog/alquiler-temporada-canarias-contrato-temporal)
- Identity: the EUDI Wallet must be offered by 24 Dec 2026, and regulated private relying parties must accept it by 24 Dec 2027. These are banking, telecoms, energy, health and very large platforms such as Booking and Amazon. Other businesses, including small landlords, are not obliged.
  - Spain presented its "Cartera Digital", built on Cl@ve (24M users), in December 2025, but "El Gobierno no ha anunciado fecha de lanzamiento". MiDNI is the DNI app "pensada para usarse en España". — [acens, 6 Oct 2026](https://blog.acens.com/actualidad/cartera-digital-europea/)
  - The pilot covered PID and age verification in May 2026. — [EADTrust, 3 Aug 2026](https://www.eadtrust.eu/blog/cartera-idue-estado-actual-en-espana/)

### Inferences
- The biggest mid-term demand segments (nomads, relocating employees, international students) are mostly foreign. They lack Spanish payslips or credit files, and today they are offset by large guarantees. RDL 29/2026 caps the extra guarantee at one month and bans requiring tenant-paid insurance, which leaves a screening and guarantee gap that software plus a landlord-paid guarantee product could fill.
- Foreign tenants cannot use a Spanish digital ID (MiDNI covers DNI holders). The EUDI Wallet will not oblige landlords, so passport plus liveness KYC remains the practical route, and it is cheap (see section 5).
- People who are moving to Spain permanently (relocating employees on indefinite contracts, "try-out" movers) arguably do not fit the "temporary displacement" cause (Albacete, A Coruña). Landlords may then refuse them or push them into rooms.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–26 nomad-visa statistics, and none by city.
- No data on international students or relocating employees per city, and no Erasmus or international enrolment figures.
- No primary source on mid-term rent levels or the seasonal premium in € per month.
- No Reddit or expat-forum evidence: searches were exhausted before those sources could be reached.

## 4. Landlord, manager and operator pains: contract choice and compliance, fianzas, registration numbers, tax, screening and insurance, turnover, utilities, pricing

### Takeaway
The day-to-day burden is now per-contract evidence and lifecycle tracking:
- writing and proving the cause;
- counting duration and consecutive contracts;
- lodging deposits under each region's rules;
- signing an end-of-lease condition record;
- answering repairs within 5 working days;
- keeping guarantees and fees inside the new caps;
- in Catalonia, also proving rent against index caps.

Tax and pricing get harder too: a VAT cliff at 30 nights from 1 Dec 2026 (if convalidated) and an IRAV ceiling on successive temporary contracts.

### Cited Findings
- Contract choice now hinges on stay length:
  - ≤31 days is tourist use (art. 5.e LAU), subject to regional tourism rules and, from 1 Dec 2026, 10% VAT for ≤30 nights;
  - 32 days–12 months is "vivienda temporal", needing a cause (art. 2.3, 7.2, 9 bis);
  - otherwise it is a habitual lease (5/7 years). — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- Fianza lodging is regional, with fines for late or missing deposits:
  - Andalucía: 5–20% late, 50–150% if not lodged;
  - C. Valenciana: 5–20% late, €600–3,000 if not lodged;
  - Madrid: 5–25% (up to €6,010) late, 26–50% (up to €90,000) if not lodged;
  - Catalonia: up to 35%, capped at €3,000;
  - Baleares: 50–75%;
  - Canarias and several other regions: 5–75%;
  - no lodging duty in Asturias, Cantabria, Murcia, Navarra or La Rioja. — [El Español, 18 Mar 2026](https://www.elespanol.com/sociedad/20260318/entrado-vigor-penalizara-caseros-no-depositen-fianza-comunidad-autonoma/1003744163850_0.html)
  - The Madrid deadline is 30 working days. — [ARAG note, 12 Nov 2024](https://www.arag.es/assets/document/20241112_ndp_-_depositar_fianza_alquiler.pdf) (search-level)
- Catalonia (INCASÒL): lodge within two months. For seasonal contracts under one year, the deposit is proportional to duration "sempre sobre la base de dues mensualitats" (Decret 147/1997). Filing is online with a digital certificate (Model 2), paid through CaixaBank, Sabadell or BBVA. — [INCASÒL, accessed 23 Oct 2026](https://incasol.gencat.cat/ca/serveis-i-tramits/fiances-de-lloguers/informacio/diposit-de-fiances)
- Canarias: lodge one month's rent with the Canarian administration within one month of the contract (Ley 2/2014), in cash at the Caja de Depósitos. Not lodging is a serious infraction fined at 35–100% of the amount. — [Lázaro Amable, 22 Oct 2026](https://lazaroamable.com/blog/alquiler-temporada-canarias-contrato-temporal). Conflicts with El Español's 5–75% for Canarias.
- Deposit filings are used as evidence in re-classification cases: in the A Coruña ruling, the filing showed annual rent of €4,800. — [idealista, 6 Jul 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/07/06/901838-la-clave-no-es-la-duracion-cuando-un-alquiler-de-temporada-pasa-a-ser-de-vivienda)
- Utilities and charges:
  - Under new art. 20, taxes on the dwelling cannot be imposed on the tenant unless the tenant is the taxpayer.
  - In tensioned zones, no new charges may be added that were not in the previous contract (art. 17.6), and large holders may not add charges that "directa o indirectamente" exceed the index cap (art. 17.7). — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
  - Requiring the tenant to register water and electricity in their own name was an indicator of a habitual lease in A Coruña. — [idealista, 6 Jul 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/07/06/901838-la-clave-no-es-la-duracion-cuando-un-alquiler-de-temporada-pasa-a-ser-de-vivienda)
  - Barcelona coliving "mandatory extras" are said to "freguen el frau de llei" (border on fraud of the law). — [Metrópoli Abierta, 2 Mar 2026](https://www.metropoliabierta.cat/el-pols-de-la-ciutat/20260302/negoci-coliving-dispara-barcelona-lombra-frau-llei-dhabitatge/1003742737508_0.html)
- Rent-default insurance (OCU, 17 policies, for a €950/month habitual lease):
  - Mutua Alquiler Confort: €306/yr for 12 months' cover, €388 for 24 months;
  - Arag: €581 (12 months), €658 (18 months);
  - Allianz €456; Mapfre €460–514; Fiatc €502; Santa Lucía €445;
  - Mutua de Propietarios (OCU deal): 2.98% of annual rent, minimum €129.78. Its separate "alquiler de temporada" product carries a Consorcio surcharge.
  - Insurers usually require annual rent ≤35–40% of the tenant's net income. OCU found "ninguna póliza completa". — [OCU, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.ocu.org/fincas-y-casas/gestion/seguros-y-prestamos/analisis/2026/07/comparativa-seguros-impago-rentas)
  - The generic market range is 3–5% of annual rent. — [Pólizamédica, Mar 2026](https://www.polizamedica.es/productos/seguro-de-impago-alquiler)
- Turnover and repairs: tenants may leave after one month with 10 days' notice (art. 11.2). Repairs must be answered within 5 working days (art. 21 bis). The end-of-lease condition document is mandatory in practice (art. 36.7). — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823)
- Administrative load: "Small owners face more management time, a higher risk of formal errors, and a growing need for specialist advice". — [idealista (Méndez Martos), 13 Jan 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/opinion/juan-ramon-mendez-martos/2026/01/13/879196-el-registro-anual-de-arrendamientos-una-nueva-obligacion-para-viviendas). Catalan agents see "un marco de inseguridad jurídica"; owners "dejarán de reformar viviendas o directamente retirarán el piso del mercado". — [idealista, 27 Jan 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/01/27/880092-regulacion-del-alquiler-de-temporada-en-catalunya-habra-un-trasvase-de-la-oferta-al)
- SES.Hospedajes (RD 933/2021, overlapping with the hospitality track): since 2 Dec 2024, lodging providers must send up to 42 data points per booking, with fines up to €30,000. — [Chekin](https://chekin.com/blog/registro-de-viajeros-ses-hospedajes-2025/). Vendor guidance says long-term lets "más de 90 días con contrato" are excluded. — [Minut](https://www.minut.com/es/blog/ses-hospedajes-guia-completa-para-cumplir-en-2025-2). How it applies to 32–90-day temporary leases is unclear.

### Inferences
- Under the new law, the landlord's best protection is a well-documented file per contract: cause, evidence, duration counter, deposit receipt, condition record, repair log and rent-cap calculation. This is a workflow and evidence problem that suits SaaS.
- Utility handling now has legal weight in two ways. Putting utilities in the tenant's name suggests habituality. All-inclusive pricing with extras risks breaching caps in tensioned zones. Transparent, allocated utility billing kept in the landlord's name is the safe pattern.

### Gaps
- No data on cleaning or turnover costs, furnishing costs (except merca2's weak capex figure) or utility costs for mid-term units.
- No data on how many landlords actually lodge seasonal deposits.
- No official guidance on whether temporary leases now carry one month's fianza (vivienda) or two (uso distinto) outside Catalonia.

## 6. Disputes and lawsuits: evictions, unpaid rent, deposits, owners' communities, sanctions

### Takeaway
Litigation focuses on whether a "seasonal" contract was really a habitual lease. Courts in Albacete (2025) and A Coruña (2026) overturned evictions for expiry and granted 5–7-year terms. National LAU evictions totalled 18,317 in 2025. The 2025 change to the Ley de Propiedad Horizontal lets owners' communities restrict tourist use (3/5 majority) but does not cover seasonal leases or coliving, which are fought through planning law instead.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 CGPJ data:
  - 25,540 evictions nationally (−11%), 18,317 of them LAU-related (71.7%) and 4,346 from mortgages;
  - Cataluña 6,814 (5,025 LAU), Andalucía 3,782 (2,600 LAU), C. Valenciana 2,992 (2,158 LAU), Madrid 2,267 (1,931 LAU). — [Forbes/CGPJ, 20 Mar 2026](https://forbes.es/economia/893652/los-desahucios-caen-un-11-en-2025-hasta-los-25-540-segun-cgpj/)

  - Rent-related evictions in Canarias fell 70.2% in Q2 2026. — [TSJ Canarias](https://www.poderjudicial.es/cgpj/es/Poder-Judicial/Tribunales-Superiores-de-Justicia/TSJ-Canarias/En-Portada/Los-lanzamientos-por-alquiler-caen-un-70-2--en-Canarias-en-el-segundo-trimestre-de-2026) (headline-level)
  - No breakdown for seasonal contracts exists.
- Eviction for "expiry" of a disguised seasonal contract fails:

Spotahome does not handle deposits — [Spotahome](https://landlord.spotahome.com/l/how-it-works) — and HousingAnywhere offers no damage insurance. — [HousingAnywhere](https://housinganywhere.com/pricing/landlords)

### Inferences
- The LPH 2025 route is a dead end for blocking mid-term or coliving uses. Neighbours' leverage over coliving is planning enforcement (major-works permits, the 30% rule) and, in Barcelona, the pending PGM change.

### Gaps
- No statistics on how many seasonal contracts are litigated or re-classified.
- No data on deposit-dispute volumes or arbitration use.
- No CGPJ split for temporary leases.
- Not re-verified: the LPH reform instrument and date. From memory it is Ley Orgánica 1/2025, in force April 2025.

## 7. Pain-point inventory (structured entries)

### Takeaway
24 distinct pains. The most severe and widespread are legal-evidentiary (cause, re-classification, rent and IRAV caps, regime volatility) and screening-related (foreign tenants under the new guarantee limits). Several look like pains but are already cheaply solved, repealed or not software-solvable (see the final section).

### HOU-01: Proving and archiving the "causa de temporalidad" for every mid-term contract
- Who: every landlord letting furnished flats or rooms for 32 days to 12 months: small owners (1–5 units), property managers and coliving operators. Most exposed are company landlords, who face 7-year terms.
- Where: national since 8 Oct 2026 (RDL 29/2026); Catalonia since 1–2 Jan 2026 (Llei 11/2025), with filing at INCASÒL.
- Day-to-day reality: before signing, the landlord must write the specific displacement cause into the contract (work posting, studies, treatment, works on the habitual home, remote work with a home abroad) and keep proof such as an enrolment letter, employer letter or foreign domicile proof. Without it, the lease is habitual from day one.
- Evidence:
  - Art. 7.2 LAU: "real y acreditable, recayendo sobre el arrendador la carga de la prueba ... con efectos retroactivos". — [BOE, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823); [idealista, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/09/30/916608-el-gobierno-obliga-a-justificar-cada-alquiler-de-temporada-y-limita-el-de)
  - Catalonia: landlord must "acrediti documentalment la causa concreta de la temporalitat". — [idealista/ca, 15 Jun 2026](https://www.idealista.com/ca/news/immobiliari/2026/06/15/901949-el-constitucional-admet-el-recurs-del-pp-contra-la-regulacio-del-lloguer-temporal-i-les)
  - Catalonia: contract, deposit and supporting documents are filed with INCASÒL. — [idealista, 4 Feb 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/02/04/881584-todo-sobre-la-ley-que-regula-el-alquiler-de-temporada-en-cataluna-y-lo-que-esta-por)
  - Document table by tenant profile; nomad cause "questionable if ... moving their life here". — [Lázaro Amable, 22 Oct 2026](https://lazaroamable.com/blog/alquiler-temporada-canarias-contrato-temporal)

## Top pains

1. **HOU-01 + HOU-13: Proving the temporary cause, and re-classification risk.** It applies to every mid-term contract nationally since 8 Oct 2026 and in Catalonia since January 2026. The penalty is a retroactive 5–7-year lease, and courts are already applying it (Albacete 2025, A Coruña 2026).
2. **HOU-03 + HOU-22 + HOU-04: Price-setting constraints.** Index caps apply to seasonal and room lets in tensioned zones (fines €3k–90k, up to €900k), an IRAV ceiling applies nationwide to successive temporary contracts, and room rents are capped at the flat's rent. Together they hit every pricing decision.
3. **HOU-02: Regulatory volatility.** Rules changed three times in 2026, the decree's survival hinges on a single vote around 12 Nov 2026, and elections follow on 29 Nov. Every operator needs contracts versioned by regime, and nobody sells that.
4. **HOU-07 + HOU-06: Screening foreign tenants under the new guarantee and insurance limits.** The core mid-term demand (nomads, relocations; 60% foreign in Barcelona coliving) collides with Spanish solvency norms just as landlords lose 6-month avales and tenant-paid insurance.
5. **HOU-05 + HOU-12: Lifecycle and deposit administration that scales with turnover.** Duration and chaining counters, one-month exits, and regional deposit filings with fines up to €90k (Madrid) apply to every short contract.
6. **HOU-09: Scams aimed at newcomers.** Police cases are frequent across regions (€80–1,100 per victim, networks with 80+ victims), and OCU showed portals do not catch fake ads.
7. **HOU-18 + HOU-04: Coliving licensing and economics.** Fast-growing supply (~19k beds, doubling by 2028) runs into Barcelona planning enforcement, the 30% rule, and room-sum caps.
8. **HOU-17: VAT cliff at 30/31 nights from 1 Dec 2026, if convalidated.** It forces per-stay tax classification for every furnished-unit operator.

## Noise and dead ends

- **Registro Único / NRUA number on listings (RD 1312/2024):** annulled by the Supreme Court (May–June 2026; effective from BOE 8 Jun 2026). Seasonal lets need no national number; the annual information model (Orden VAU/1560/2025) is tied to it. Only a one-off refund-claim niche remains (>€20M in fees; 1-year window). Regional tourist registries (Canarias, Baleares, Catalonia) concern ≤31-day lets, which is hospitality-track territory.
- **PL 122/000136 (national seasonal-rental bill):** reportedly rejected on 26 Feb 2026 (single source) and in any case lapses with the dissolution. Superseded by RDL 29/2026.
- **RDL 26/2026 and RDL 27/2026:** derogated on 2 Oct 2026 after one or two days in force. RDL 29/2026 itself may lapse around 12 Nov 2026, so any product built only on it is high-risk. Catalonia's Llei 11/2025 is the durable anchor.
- **Ley de Propiedad Horizontal 3/5 rule (2025):** covers tourist activity (art. 5.e LAU, ≤31 days), not seasonal leases or coliving. It is not a lever against mid-term uses, and sources disagree on whether it even allows outright prohibition.
- **SES.Hospedajes for mid-term stays:** an obligation for lodging; vendor guides exclude contracts over 90 days. It is ambiguous for 32–90-day temporary leases, weakly evidenced and belongs to the hospitality track.
- **Standalone tenant KYC / identity-verification SaaS:** already cheap (Didit offers 500 free checks per month and $0.33 per applicant). The EUDI wallet will not oblige landlords (private acceptance by Dec 2027 only for regulated sectors and very large platforms), and Spain's wallet has no launch date. It works only as a component.
- **Dynamic pricing for temporary leases:** legally neutralised by the art. 17.8 IRAV ceiling (and caps in Catalonia) while RDL 29/2026 stands. It remains relevant only for ≤31-day tourist lets (hospitality track) or if the decree lapses.
- **"Barcelona bans seasonal rentals" headlines:** the PGM change was only initially approved on 27 Mar 2026 and needs Generalitat approval plus a special plan. It is a draft, not law. Tourinews' claim that "the council capped seasonal prices in Dec 2025" misattributes the Parlament's Llei 11/2025.
- **Coliving market-size and yield claims from blogs** (merca2, Funciohouse, investor blogs: "98% occupancy", "8–11% yields", "60% of operators fail"): unattributed. Use JLL (19,089 beds) and treat the rest as low confidence.
- **Digital-nomad visa counts:** single unverified source (27,875 by end-2024) with no city split. Not usable for sizing per city.
